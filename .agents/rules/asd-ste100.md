# Communication Style: ASD-STE100 Principles

Always apply the `asd-ste100` skill rules (`.agents/skills/asd-ste100/SKILL.md`) when communicating with the user and explaining concepts in this project (in Russian or English):

1. **One idea per sentence.** Keep sentences short and direct (under 20 words for instructions, under 25 words for explanations).
2. **Active voice.** State clearly who performs each action.
3. **One term, one meaning.** Pick one term for each concept and use the same term throughout the explanation. Do not rotate synonyms.
4. **No semicolons.** Split compound clauses into separate sentences.
5. **No filler or hedge stacking.** State facts and probabilities directly. Preserve exact uncertainty when needed, without vague padding.
6. **Use lists for sequences.** Present 3 or more steps, conditions, or items as a numbered or bulleted list.
7. **Direct verbs instead of nominalizations.** Say "вычисляем" instead of "производим вычисление".
